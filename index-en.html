Our Mission

Not like the other associations.

We help those who want to do things right — with responsibility, respect, and a real desire to integrate into French society.

Sur la Bonne Voie was born from direct experience with the French system — its strengths, its flaws, and what nobody tells you when you arrive. We share what we have learned, for free, so others can move faster and more surely.

Our approach is simple: practical knowledge, real examples, and truth without filters.

Responsibility before rights
Integration is earned through action. We help those who show up.
Lived experience only
Every piece of advice comes from someone who has lived it — not from a manual.
Mutual respect
We respect France, its laws and its culture. We ask for the same in return.
No gray areas
We call things by their name. Real integration looks like something specific.
What we believe

Our manifesto — no filters.

01
Thinking clearly is not racism.
Calling things by their name is not an act of hate — it is an act of respect toward those who do things right. Refusing this clarity means abandoning honest people.
02
Nothing is free. Nothing is impossible.
Neither papers, nor work, nor friendships, nor trust. Everything must be earned. Everything must be built. But nothing is impossible if you put in the necessary effort.
03
France owes you nothing. You owe everything to France.
Coming to France is a chance. In return, the immigrant owes France gratitude, respect, and a promise that his presence will make this country better.
04
The past explains. It does not excuse.
We live in 2026, not in 1926. There is only one reality — no matter how you feel about it, it does not change. What changes is what you decide to do with it.

For the French

You love your country. So do we.

"What you do is exactly what we hope for." — What many French people regularly tell us.

This is not a compliment. It is a legitimate expectation that nobody takes seriously. You love your country. You worry about what is happening to it. Wanting those who come here to respect France — its laws, its language, its customs — is a normal expectation. We fully share it.

Sur la Bonne Voie only helps those who share these values. This is our commitment to you.

Our Story

We didn't come here to wait.

Ardèche, France
Since 2022

"Sur la Bonne Voie was born from lived experience — not from an office, not from funding, not from theory."

We arrived in France without speaking a word of French, without contacts, without resources. Our asylum application was rejected. We did not wait for someone to come and save us.

We learned French — up to B2+ level, certified, in less than two years, as self-learners. We built relationships in our village, organized events, and helped isolated immigrants recover a social life. We navigated hundreds of administrative procedures, met mayors, judges, and prefectures.

"We are still regularizing our own administrative situation. This means every guide we write comes from someone who is living the process today — not from someone who has already forgotten it."

Sur la Bonne Voie exists because this knowledge should not stay with us alone. And because waiting for permission to help others was never an option.

Our papers are not yet final. Our integration is real.
